Risk management and quality

The risks the food system actually runs here

The register is about pandemic food security in this valley, including a pandemic that arrives while the program is still being built. Quality is the set of tests that keep a reserve from becoming a liability.

Risk method

Risk is the effect of uncertainty on objectives (International Organization for Standardization [ISO], 2018b). FoodShield treats pandemic risk in the sense public-health guidance already uses: a severe respiratory event that stresses health services, workforces, and supply chains together (Public Health Agency of Canada, 2018; World Health Organization, 2017). It also treats compound events, because this valley has recent evidence that floods and landslides can isolate communities at the same time as any other crisis (Gillett et al., 2022). The Sendai Framework’s emphasis on understanding risk before a disaster, and on protecting essential services, is the policy context (United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction, 2015). It is not a claim that this classroom project implements the framework.

Scores use a five-point likelihood and a five-point severity. Likelihood is judged over the project life and the first years of operation, not over a single week. Severity is judged by the effect on safe food access, not by how embarrassing the headline would be.

  • Likelihood: 1 rare, 2 unlikely, 3 possible, 4 likely, 5 almost certain.
  • Severity: 1 negligible, 2 minor, 3 moderate, 4 major, 5 severe. Severe means a break in safe food for priority populations, a critical spoilage, or a failure of activation.
  • Score: likelihood times severity. 15 to 25 is high and is reviewed monthly. 8 to 14 is medium and is reviewed at each quarterly gate. Below 8 is kept on the long register, which is not printed here.

Responses are avoid, mitigate, transfer or share, or accept. Acceptance is allowed only when it is written down, assigned to the sponsor, and paired with a trigger that would reopen the decision. Cost inflation and estimate error are real project risks; they are handled on the cost page so this table can stay a pandemic-risk register, as the brief requires.

R5 Distribution is unequal, or is believed to be unequal. Priority populations wait while better-connected households clear the community point. Provide an appeal path that works during an activation, not only afterwards. Accessibility is a control, not a communications theme (Accessible British Columbia Act, 2021).
R6 Cold chain or the grid fails during a heat event, a winter outage, or both. Chilled stock is lost in the week it is most needed. 3 5 15 Mitigate. Shelf-stable food is the core reserve so the mission survives a refrigeration failure. N+1 refrigeration, backup power, and temperature alarms that do not depend on the logistics platform. Temperature mapping is an acceptance test. Spoiled product has a disposal path that does not get mixed back into inventory.
R7 A serious animal-health emergency, including highly pathogenic avian influenza in a poultry-dense valley, runs at the same time as a human pandemic. Protein supply drops, and public fear treats all local food as suspect. 3 4 12 Share and mitigate. Disease control stays with the Canadian Food Inspection Agency and producers. FoodShield’s control is a protein substitution in the reserve, a message that distinguishes a farm-level animal disease from the safety of unrelated foods, and a recall drill. Eradication programs and compensation for culled animals stay outside scope.
R8 Panic buying empties retail before any formal emergency declaration. By the time activation criteria written only for a declared state are met, the 72-hour household buffer is already gone. 4 3 12 Mitigate. Activation triggers include leading indicators agreed with Fraser Health and local emergency managers, not only a single legal instrument. Public communication says what the reserve is for and what it will not do, so the project does not accidentally start a run on the hubs. Retail hoarding is not solved by a larger warehouse alone; the rule has to be visible.
R9 The logistics platform, or the identity system around it, is compromised during an emergency. Stock records lie, personal information leaks, or alarms are silenced. 3 4 12 Mitigate. Canadian data residency, segmentation, a security test before M5, and a paper-and-radio procedure exercised with the network disconnected. Temperature alarms have an independent path. A platform outage is a degraded mode, not a reason to stop feeding people. Privacy incidents follow the public-sector privacy statute (Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act, 1996).
R10 Municipalities and the health authority disagree about who may activate, and the argument happens in the first week of a wave rather than in a 2028 tabletop. 3 4 12 Mitigate. A signed activation protocol is an M2 exit criterion, even though M2 is formally a baseline gate. The tabletop’s scenario is a disagreement, not a parade. The scenario champion may break a deadlock inside the classroom fiction; the protocol also names the real legal authority under the emergency statute so the fiction is not the plan (Emergency and Disaster Management Act, 2023).

Likelihood (L) and severity (S) are on scales of 1 to 5. Scores of 15 and above are high. All ten risks are at least medium, which is why they are the top ten and not a sample of minor irritants. Residual risk above the 90-day priority reserve is accepted by the sponsor and restated in the charter: this program does not feed every resident for an unlimited time.

How responses are owned

Each risk has one owner, named in planning when posts are filled. Until then, the program director owns the register and the food-safety lead owns R6 and R7’s food-safety controls. Owners do not “watch” a risk. They either fund the control, schedule it, or bring an acceptance statement to the sponsor. The register is updated monthly and whenever a trigger fires: a flood warning, a border measure, a plant closure, a cyber alert, or a two-month cost-performance slide that would push the reserve fill past the exercise.

Secondary risks are written down when a control creates them. Pre-positioning at Hope increases the chance of a smaller site being understaffed. The control for that secondary risk is the cross-trained roster, not a larger Hope building. Publishing the allocation rule may anger households that are not on the priority list. That anger is accepted. Hiding the rule would trade a political inconvenience for R5, which is the worse risk.

Quality management plan

Quality here means fitness for the charter’s purpose: safe food, delivered under the published rule, by people and equipment that still work when conditions are bad. It is not a claim of ISO certification on day one. The program uses ISO 9001 as a management pattern — customer focus, process approach, evidence-based decisions, and improvement — without pretending a certificate is the objective (ISO, 2015). Food safety uses the preventive-control logic in the Codex general principles of food hygiene, including HACCP, and the Canadian regulations that apply to the suppliers and to the operation (Codex Alimentarius Commission, 2020; Safe Food for Canadians Regulations, 2018). Business continuity for activation uses the ideas in ISO 22301, especially that a business-continuity plan untested is a document, not a capability (ISO, 2018c). Food-safety management system ideas in ISO 22000 inform supplier control (ISO, 2018a).

The quality lead can stop a release, a first fill, or an exercise credit. Stopping a milestone is a decision the change board must hear within five business days. The lead cannot be overruled by the program director alone if the issue is a critical food-safety non-conformance or a privacy breach. Those go to the sponsor with a recommended disposition: correct, waive with a time limit, or descale.

Acceptance is evidence, not attendance. A signed ribbon-cutting photograph is not a temperature map, a disconnect test, or a roster drill run at 30 percent absence.

Quality objectives tied to acceptance

  • No critical food-safety non-conformance open when a room is filled.
  • Every reserve lot traceable to a supplier and a location within the time the recall procedure states.
  • Backup power carries the reserve rooms for the duration stated in the design criteria, demonstrated on load, not on a calculator.
  • The allocation rule used in the software is the same text published to the public.
  • The offline exercise day completes the script without the wide-area network.
  • Public-facing spaces and information meet the accessibility commitments written into the partnership package.

Testing stages

Physical systems and software do not share a single test vocabulary. They share a rule: the next stage does not start to “save the date” while a critical defect in the current stage is open. Date pressure is real, and the descoping ladder is how the date is saved.

Table 16. Test and exercise sequence
Stage Applies to What must be true to pass
Design qualification Hubs, refrigeration, fleet specification, platform architecture The design can meet the charter: flood and seismic criteria, N+1 cold rooms, duty cycle, offline mode, privacy minimization. Review is minuted. The prototype hub is not copied until its design-qualification findings are closed.
Installation qualification Equipment as installed The equipment on the slab is the equipment that was specified, with calibration certificates for sensors and a spare-parts list the receiver can actually buy.
Operational qualification Cold rooms, backup power, docks, vehicles Systems run across the stated range. Backup power starts under load. A truck completes the Hope run in winter conditions or the mixed-powertrain decision is formally made.
Performance qualification Cold rooms and the reserve process Temperature mapping under a realistic load. Alarms reach a human when the platform is switched off. A mock recall finds the lot inside the procedure’s time limit.
Spiral reviews Digital platform Each spiral ends with working software or a deliberate decision not to build. Defects are triaged. New scope discovered in review goes to the backlog or to the change board, not straight into the sprint to be helpful.
Security and privacy test Platform, identity, hosting The security test agreed in the contract is executed before M5. The privacy impact assessment is closed or has a time-limited waiver from the sponsor. Production data are not used as test data.
Seminar and tabletop Decision-makers 2028. The tabletop scenario is an activation dispute plus a road closure. Success is a decision, a record, and a revised protocol, not consensus theatre.
Functional exercise One hub, a subset of the fleet, the platform Early 2029. Moves real practice stock. Includes a recall and a shift change. Critical defects block the full-scale exercise until closed or waived.
Full-scale exercise The system as the public will meet it By September 30, 2029. Draws stock, rolls fleet to community points in more than one municipality, runs with the network disconnected for a scripted period, and runs with a pre-briefed absence level. The quality lead writes the pass or fail. A fail does not get renamed a “learning success” in the public note. It gets a defect list and a handover decision.

Reporting cadence

Reports are short enough that a sponsor will read them. They use the same definitions every month so a trend is visible. Narrative colour without the indices is not a control report (PMI, 2017).
