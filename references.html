A note on use

Population counts for Abbotsford (153,524) and Chilliwack (93,203) are taken from the 2021 Census release (Statistics Canada, 2022). The working total of 320,000 residents, and the 73,273-person remainder inside it, are planning assumptions. They are to be replaced by a locked census extract before any reserve is tendered. Secondary compilations of Fraser Valley population do not always match census-subdivision totals, which is why this proposal does not mix them into the arithmetic.

Carney (2021) is cited only for its argument about value beyond immediate market price. It is not a source for the CAD $1,000,000,000 figure. That figure, the task force, and every facility upgrade in the budget are hypothetical, as the footer on each page states. Web addresses were recorded for this draft and should be rechecked against the issuing organization’s catalogue if a link moves.
