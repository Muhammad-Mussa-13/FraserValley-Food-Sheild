Cost, budget, and procurement

A fixed billion dollars, fully allocated

Every dollar of the fictional ceiling is assigned. The total is CAD $1,000,000,000. Nothing in the quality or payback discussion is an extra amount on top of that ceiling.

Basis of estimate

The estimate is a scenario baseline in constant 2027 Canadian dollars. It is not a Class 1 estimate from quantity takeoffs, because there are no completed designs. Inflation between 2027 and 2029 is not hidden inside the lines. It is a risk. Contracts may include an economic price adjustment only where the solicitation names the index and the cap. Otherwise, price growth is paid from contingency or removed from scope.

Two reserves follow the usual project distinction. The contingency reserve, held by the project manager, covers identified risks that may or may not occur, sometimes called known-unknowns. The management reserve, held by the scenario sponsor, covers unplanned work inside the charter purpose, sometimes called unknown-unknowns (PMI, 2017). Neither reserve is a place to hide scope that was deliberately excluded, such as post-2029 operations or clinical care.

Staff costs in the workforce lines are operational: hub, fleet, food safety, and training. The project-management line is the project management office, controls, procurement administration, and legal support. Counting both is not double counting if those roles stay distinct. The office shrinks at handover. The operating staff may transfer, and their later salaries are a receiver cost.

Procurement strategy

No vendor is named as the winner in this proposal. Naming a winner before an open process would be a finding, not a plan. Strategy is organized by how well the scope can be specified, which is also why the lifecycle split matters for contract type (PMI, 2017).

HVAC, cold storage, and hub construction

The Abbotsford hub is procured as a design-bid-build prototype, or as a design competition followed by a build tender, so the owner still understands the building it is about to repeat. Later hubs may use design-build against that frozen performance specification if the prototype’s defects are closed. A guaranteed maximum price is acceptable only with an open-book schedule of values and a change process the project manager actually has the staff to review. Refrigeration performance includes redundancy for reserve rooms, independent temperature alarms, and a stated refrigerant choice. The alarm path is specified to keep reporting when the logistics platform is down, because a silent cooler is a food-safety event. Fairness monitoring is required on awards of this size. Evaluation criteria publish the weight given to safety record, energy performance, local service capacity, and price, before bids are opened.

Electric and mixed fleet

The duty-cycle study is an owner document, finished before the vehicle solicitation. It states range in winter, charge time at the depot, grade toward Hope, and the payload of a refrigerated body. If a battery-electric heavy unit fails that study, the Hope run is specified as a hybrid or conventional unit rather than accepted on a manufacturer’s mild-weather claim. Vehicle awards are firm fixed price per equipped unit after specifications are frozen. An economic price adjustment is allowed only for a named index. Charging and depot works are a separate lot, so the truck manufacturer does not own the plug. Telematics data are exportable at handover. Receivers will not be left inside a proprietary diagnostic tool they cannot replace.

Cloud platform and software

A fixed-price waterfall contract is a poor fit for the early spirals, because the risky work is still being discovered (Boehm, 1988). Development uses a capped time-and-materials or target-cost form through Spirals 1 to 4, with a fixed fee for Spiral 5 hardening so the incentive at the end is stability, not new features. Hosting is a separate service order: a supplier that can keep the production environment in a Canadian region, with an exit clause, a data-export format, and a prohibition on using personal information for the supplier’s own product training. The Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act (1996) is the design constraint for any personal information in a public body’s custody. The platform contract also requires the paper-and-radio procedure to be delivered and tested. Software that cannot be turned off without stopping food movement has failed acceptance, regardless of its demonstration on a connected network.

Food reserves

Reserves are bought through multi-vendor standing offers, not a single-supplier bargain. Lots must be traceable under the Safe Food for Canadians Regulations (2018) where those rules apply, and under contract conditions where they do not. Rotation is an operating procedure with a named loss factor, not a hope that someone will remember expiry dates. Recall clauses are exercised in the functional exercise, including a recall that arrives on a Friday afternoon.

Cost of quality

Feigenbaum’s cost-of-quality model sorts spending into prevention, appraisal, and failure (Feigenbaum, 1991). Crosby’s claim that quality is free is best read as a claim that prevention is cheaper than external failure, not as a claim that the quality budget should be zero (Crosby, 1979). FoodShield makes external failure the expensive case: a foodborne illness from a public reserve, or a cooler that spoils the 90-day stock in a heat event, would erase more value than the audit line.

The balance is intentional. A quality program that spends more on finding defects than on preventing them is inspecting a design it should have changed. The independent quality lead can report an unresolved non-conformance to the sponsor without asking the program director for permission. That reporting path is part of the cost of quality, because a quality function that can be silenced by the schedule has already failed.

Return and payback

A public food-security asset does not throw off a sales margin. The honest test is avoided loss and the annual cost of holding the option, set beside an operating cost the project will not pay after 2029. The cases below are illustrations. They are not forecasts, and they are not a Treasury Board direction. They exist so a reader can see what would have to be true for the billion dollars to be proportionate.

Table 10. Illustrative value cases, using the 96,000-person priority assumption
Case Assumption, stated so it can be attacked Result
One acute wave, household view only 96,000 people avoid 30 days of emergency replacement at $25 per person-day. $72,000,000 of avoided household cost in that wave. This does not recover $1,000,000,000.
Expected-value public case $80,000,000 a year in probability-weighted avoided loss, counting health externalities, spoilage, and emergency logistics, before operating cost. Simple payback of 12.5 years if the assumption holds every year, which it will not. Quiet years pay nothing back. Severe years pay more.
After operating cost Receivers fund about $20,000,000 a year after handover for staff, energy, a vehicle sinking fund, rotation losses, and exercises. That allowance is outside the ceiling and must be replaced by a real operating budget before acceptance. Net $60,000,000 a year under the expected-value case. Simple payback of about 17 years.
Capital recovery A 3 percent real discount rate over a 25-year hub life. Annual recovery is P × [r(1+r)n] / [(1+r)n − 1]. With (1.03)25 ≈ 2.094, the payment is about $57 million a year, and it excludes operations. If society’s expected avoided loss, net of operating cost, stays below that annuity, the investment is not justified on this test. A higher discount rate makes the test harder.

The household case is the one a critic should start with, because it is the easiest to defend and it is not enough. The argument for the larger figure depends on the valley’s role as a producer and on compound failure of the kind Gillett et al. (2022) document for 2021: isolation, not only empty shelves. This proposal does not invent a farm-cash-receipt total to force the arithmetic over the line. If a later study can document expected agricultural and health losses with sources, it should replace Table 10 rather than decorate it.

There is also an option reading, consistent with the wider idea of value in Carney (2021), used here only as an intellectual reference. The hubs are a capability that is exercised in bad years. Option value is not an excuse to skip the annuity test. It is a reason to keep the shelf-stable core and the Hope hub when someone proposes to spend the same money on features that work only on a calm, connected afternoon.

Cost risks and the descoping ladder

Eight percent combined reserve is still modest for floodplain construction and a food-commodity first fill. The compensating control is an ordered list of what is given up first. The board uses it when the cost performance index on hubs stays below 1.0 and the recovery plan cannot return the package to baseline without new scope or a new date.

  1. Defer platform features that are not required for inventory truth, the published allocation rule, or the offline day.
  2. Reduce the last-mile van count and use partner vehicles under agreement.
  3. Defer satellite fit-out in Harrison Hot Springs and in electoral-area points. Those points remain served from primary hubs.
  4. Reduce chilled-buffer variety before touching the shelf-stable 90-day core.
  5. Do not cut, in order to save the date: food-safety systems, independent temperature alarms, backup power for reserve rooms, the Hope reserve room, accessibility and language supports, Indigenous consent obligations, the full-scale exercise, the independent audit, or the receiver operating budget required for handover.

If protected scope cannot be completed inside the ceiling and the date, the honest act is to descale a whole hub’s non-reserve finishes or to report that the scenario cannot meet its own charter. It is not to quietly drop the exercise or to hand over a building with no one funded to run it.
